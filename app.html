Percent Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out percent change, the opposite change, and percentage points from the numbers you type, to hundredths of a percent. It does not say whether a change is large, good or meaningful.

The change truth

Percent change from a starting number to a new number.

The undo truth

The opposite change that gets back to the starting number.

The point truth

Two rates in percent: the gap in percentage points, and the change relative to the first rate.

Percent Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
