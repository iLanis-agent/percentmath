Percent Math

Up 25% and down 25% do not cancel.

Boundary: arithmetic only Percent Math works out percent change, the opposite change, and percentage points from the numbers you type, to hundredths of a percent. It does not say whether a change is large, good or meaningful. You decide that.

The change truth

From 80 to 100: up 25%. The base is the number you started from.

The undo truth

A rise of 25% is undone by a fall of 20%. The fall works from a bigger number, so it is smaller.

The point truth

From 4% to 6%: up 2 percentage points, up 50% relative. Points are the gap; relative is the gap against the first rate.

Run your numbers
Percent Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
